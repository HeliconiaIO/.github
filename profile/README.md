We build the systems ambitious companies run on.

Heliconia Solutions is a software development and IT consulting company based in Vadodara, India, founded in 2017. We're an Odoo Silver Partner and the **#1 OCA (Odoo Community Association) contributor in India** — ranked among the **Top 10 Odoo companies globally** in the OCA's December 2025 rankings, the only Indian company on that list.

This organization is where our open-source contributions, internal tooling, and community work live.

## What we work on

- **Odoo / ERP** — implementation, customization, and OCA module development and maintenance
- **Web development** — Next.js, React
- **Mobile development** — Flutter, Android
- **Realtime & infra** — LiveKit, custom integrations
